# Privacy Policy — PermCheck

_Last updated: 2026-07-26_

PermCheck does not collect, store off-device, transmit, sell, or share any personal
or usage data.

**What it accesses:** the list of extensions installed in your own browser and their
declared permissions, via the Chrome `management` API (read-only).

**What it stores:** a snapshot of your extensions' permissions in `chrome.storage.local`
on your own device, used solely to detect when an extension gains new access between
scans. This never leaves your computer and is never synced.

**What it sends:** nothing. PermCheck makes no network requests of any kind.

**Third parties:** none. There is no analytics, no telemetry, no external service.

**Contact:** https://github.com/keets-zero

Because PermCheck transmits no data, there is nothing to request, export, or delete
beyond uninstalling the extension (which clears its local storage).
